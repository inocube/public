# Goals

## Why the site exists

Turn visitors (IT managers, CFOs, COOs and innovation leads at SK/CZ financial institutions and mid-size
firms) into booked 30-minute consultations. The site confirms credibility when someone hears about Inocube
through contacts or LinkedIn.

## What the site must show

- Positioning: AI and data connected to the client's existing systems, secure and AI Act compliant.
- Three service packages: AI agent, Data and reporting, Custom applications and modernisation.
- Four anonymised case studies.
- How cooperation works (workshop → proof of concept → production → operation).
- Full contact details and company identifiers (e-mail, phone, address, IČO, DIČ), team/owner with LinkedIn.
- A clear call to action: book a free 30-minute consultation.

## Success criteria

- At least 1 inbound enquiry per month from the site within 90 days, 4+ within 12 months.
- Lighthouse ≥ 95 on the home page; fully usable on mobile.
- Slovak first; English version for CZ and DACH visitors in a later phase.

## Out of scope

- Blog/CMS, e-shop, client portal, paid advertising landing pages.
